Our approach

One supplier for
the whole basket

Most households do not shop by category. They buy the water, the beans, the kitchen towel and the bin bags in the same trip — so we built a brand that covers all of it.

That means a retailer can stock three aisles from a single supplier, on one invoice and one delivery. And it means a shopper who trusts the RAHA water has a reason to trust the RAHA towel next to it.

  • Consistent quality standards applied across every category
  • Packaging designed to be recognised from across the aisle
  • Trade formats that palletise, stack and stock-take cleanly

What we stand for

Three things we will not trade away

01

Household first

Every decision starts with the person paying at the till. Sizes, formats and formulas are chosen for real budgets and real kitchens — not for a marketing deck.

02

Packaging that can come back

PET bottles, steel cans and paper products across the range are chosen so the packaging can be recovered and recycled rather than simply thrown away.

03

South African first

We favour South African suppliers wherever the quality and the timing allow it, and we build the range around what local households actually buy.

How it reaches you

From source to shelf

Nothing exotic — just a short, well-controlled chain with checks at each handover. It is the reason a case ordered in March looks and tastes exactly like a case ordered in November.

  1. Source & supplySpring water drawn at source; food and household inputs bought to a fixed specification.
  2. Fill & packBottling, canning and packing under batch control, with tamper-evident closures on every bottle.
  3. Batch checksEach batch is checked and recorded before it is released to the warehouse.
  4. Case & palletiseSingles, six-packs and full cases built to stack squarely and survive the road.
  5. DistributeOut to wholesalers, retailers and food-service customers nationwide.

Common questions

Before you write

Do you sell directly to the public?

RAHA is a house brand supplied through retailers, wholesalers and food-service customers. If you are a shopper looking for stock near you, send us your area and we will point you to the closest stockist.

What is the minimum order for trade?

Orders are built in full cases — 24 × 500 ml for water, 12 × 400 g for baked beans, and so on. Minimums depend on your delivery region, so tell us where you are and we will confirm.

Can I order a mixed load across categories?

Yes. That is the point of a single house brand — water, food and household disposables can travel on one order and one invoice.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver nationwide. Lead times vary by province and load size; we confirm both when we quote.

How quickly do you reply?

Within one business day for enquiries received Monday to Friday. WhatsApp is usually the fastest route.
